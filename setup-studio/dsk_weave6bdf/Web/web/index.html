Welcome

This port stands on the work of many people. Thank you to everyone whose work and ideas made it possible, including the artists, programmers, writers and scientists behind the modern tools it was built with. Ideas, code and AI should be free, shared and open to everyone.

Thank you to the developers of The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess for a game that is still loved today.

Thank you to the community around the game, especially the developers of Dusklight and everyone who worked on the decompilation, the PS5 port, DolphinPS4 and Twilight HD.

This port exists because of their work. Have a lot of fun with it.

What you need

  • The game files: dump your own disc (guide)
  • The textures: Henriko's download page (the HD texture pack and the PlayStation buttons addon are both on this page)

What the script does

  • Runs on Linux, Windows, and macOS, with a GUI
  • Asks which language you want and whether to install improved textures
  • Builds a ready-to-install package

If you enjoy it, please consider supporting the developers mentioned above; any amount helps. Feedback about bugs, errors, misconfigurations or possible improvements is welcome.

Have fun, and good luck!

Game data belongs to Nintendo and the texture pack's author asks that it is only downloaded from his page, so you bring both yourself. Everything stays on your computer and your PS4; nothing is uploaded anywhere else.

Choose your game

Twilight Princess for GameCube or for Wii: both work. Choose the .iso of your own disc.

GameCube

Works directly. Nothing else needed.

GZ2E01 (USA) · GZ2P01 (Europe, 5 languages) · GZ2J01 (Japan)

Wii

A decrypted image works directly. An encrypted dump (as CleanRip makes it) needs your Wii key: add it below.

RZDE01 (USA) · RZDP01 (Europe, 5 languages) · RZDJ01 (Japan)

or drag the file into this window

How to dump your own disc with CleanRip · CleanRip

Wii dump? Add your Wii key

Only needed for an encrypted Wii dump. Paste your Wii's common key, or choose the keys.bin from your own Wii's BootMii backup. The key is only used to decrypt your disc on this computer and is never saved.

or

The Setup Studio contains no Nintendo keys. BootMii writes keys.bin together with its NAND backup (nand.bin) to the SD card; the common key is also shown by tools that read keys.bin.

How to make a BootMii backup (keys.bin)

HD textures (optional)

Henriko's texture pack makes the game sharper. Download the 1080p version for Dusklight (PC) from his page, then choose the ZIP or its extracted folder here.

Tip: drag the texture pack and the button addon (folders or ZIPs) into this window, together or one by one.
